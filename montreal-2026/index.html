Yoon Family Vacation

Montreal

August 19-25, 2026: a relaxed city-and-nature itinerary based at Sofitel Montreal Golden Mile, with one strong half-day plan for each full day and plenty of open space around meals.

ATL to YUL: Wed Aug 19, evening arrival Hotel: Sofitel Montreal Golden Mile Return: Tue Aug 25, midday flight

Home Base

Sofitel Montreal Golden Mile is a convenient downtown anchor for this trip: close to Mount Royal, the Montreal Museum of Fine Arts, McGill, Sherbrooke Street, Sainte-Catherine Street, and easy rides to Old Montreal, Jean-Talon Market, and the Botanical Garden.

Best rhythmOne planned activity block per day, then open afternoons and evenings.
Neighborhood mixOld Montreal, Mile End, Plateau, Golden Square Mile, and the Old Port.
Nature dayMont Saint-Hilaire for a beautiful hike within easy driving range.
Travel styleAdult-family pace: food, views, walking, culture, and one restorative spa option.

Day By Day

The full days each have a main outing that should fit comfortably into about half a day. The one exception is Friday, which is reserved for the nature excursion.

Wed Aug 19

Arrival Night

Land at YUL at 11:52pm on DL2416. Keep the first night simple: pre-book a car or large taxi, check in, and sleep.

Travel dayNo plans

Thu Aug 20

Old Montreal + Waterfront

Late morning in Old Montreal: Notre-Dame Basilica, Place Jacques-Cartier, cobblestone streets, and the Old Port waterfront. Optional evening: AURA at Notre-Dame for a polished, low-effort night activity.

Old MontrealNotre-DameWaterfront

Fri Aug 21

Nature Excursion: Mont Saint-Hilaire

Leave after breakfast for Gault Nature Reserve. Hike around Lac Hertel and choose a summit route depending on energy. Return to Montreal mid to late afternoon.

Day tripForestSummit views

Sat Aug 22

Jean-Talon Market + Mile End

Start with coffee, fruit, pastries, cheese, and market browsing. Continue into Little Italy and Mile End for bagels, cafes, shops, and a relaxed food-forward day.

MarketLittle ItalyMile End

Sun Aug 23

Mount Royal + Plateau

Walk or ride partway up Mount Royal for Kondiaronk Belvedere, the chalet, and Beaver Lake. Follow with Plateau wandering, murals, boutiques, and cafes.

Mon Aug 24

Botanical Garden + Bota Bota

Spend the morning at the Montreal Botanical Garden. In the late afternoon, consider Bota Bota in the Old Port for a water circuit and a calm final evening before departure day.

Tue Aug 25

Departure

DL2629 departs YUL at 12:44pm. Plan to leave the hotel around 9:00-9:15am unless Delta advises a different airport timing.

Travel dayYUL to ATL

Best Nature Day

Gault Nature Reserve at Mont Saint-Hilaire is the best fit for a beautiful outing within the requested driving radius. It gives the family real forest, a lake, summit options, and a strong change of scenery without sacrificing the whole day to logistics.

Suggested Friday Plan

  • 8:30am: leave Sofitel Montreal Golden Mile.
  • 9:30am: arrive, check in, start hiking.
  • Choose a moderate route around Lac Hertel with one summit if weather is good.
  • 1:30pm: late lunch near Mont-Saint-Hilaire or back in Montreal.
  • Mid/late afternoon: return, shower, easy dinner.
